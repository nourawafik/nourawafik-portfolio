A web platform US mental health clinics use to run their day: appointments, patients, providers, billing and claims, and reports. It serves four roles (admin, provider, patient and staff) and is live in the US across more than 50 clinics. I'm the only designer on the product.

<NdaNote />

<Section title="One product, four roles">

Admins and providers share the same navigation, components and data, but each lands on a different home. The admin sees the whole operation and what needs attention. The provider sees today's sessions and the next patient.

<Section title="Patient profile">

Everything a provider checks before a session, on one screen: next appointment, diagnoses, medications and notes in order. Anything written by AI is labelled and shows who approved it.

<DesktopScreenshot src="/work/praxa/patient.png" srcDark="/work/praxa/patient-dark.png" alt="Profile overview, with AI content labelled as approved" caption="Profile overview, with AI content labelled as approved" />

</Section>

<Section title="AI the provider approves">

AI drafts the diagnosis from the session. Each section shows its source, the provider accepts or edits it section by section, and nothing reaches the chart until every section is reviewed.

<DesktopScreenshot src="/work/praxa/aidx.png" srcDark="/work/praxa/aidx-dark.png" alt="Every section shows its source, and approval waits until all are reviewed" caption="Every section shows its source, and approval waits until all are reviewed" />

</Section>
