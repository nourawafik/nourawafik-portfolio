Ulfa

A bilingual Arabic and English mobile app that brings people together with licensed mental health facilitators, in small circles and structured sessions.

My role
Product design and product management
Platform
Mobile app · Arabic and English
Scope
Participant and facilitator journeys
The name and details are changed, and every screen here was redrawn for this case study.

The challenge

One product, two audiences: participants who need to feel safe from the first screen, and facilitators who need to run their schedule and earnings without friction. All of it in two languages, on a subject as sensitive as mental health.

1

Calm and readable

Quiet colors, with contrast above AA in light and dark.

2

Bilingual from day one

Every screen designed in Arabic and English, not translated later.

3

Consent before you join

Rules and privacy are read before the session, not inside it.
